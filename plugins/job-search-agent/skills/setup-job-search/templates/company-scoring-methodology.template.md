# Company Scoring Methodology

## How scoring works

1. Run the deal-breaker screen from dream-job-criteria.md first. A company that fails it is not scored. Its category columns stay blank.
2. If it passes, score each category from 0 to 10 using the anchors below.
3. Total = sum of (category score x weight) divided by 10. Weights are points out of 100, so the total lands on a 0 to 100 scale.
4. Set the Score Band and the Status from the table at the bottom.

## Categories and weights

Setup adjusts these to your priorities. The weights must add up to 100. If you change the names or weights, rebuild the tracker headers so they match.

| # | Category | Weight | A 10 looks like | A 5 looks like | A 0 looks like |
|---|----------|--------|-----------------|----------------|----------------|
| 1 | Culture & Leadership | 12 | Leaders named and respected, stable team, consistent positive review themes | Mixed signals or thin data | Repeated toxic-management or turnover themes |
| 2 | Autonomy & Org Influence | 12 | Reports to a top executive, owns budget and hiring | Reports one level down, shared budget | Execution-only seat, decisions made elsewhere |
| 3 | Compensation | 12 | Base at or above target, equity meaningful | At the floor | Below the floor, no flexibility |
| 4 | Flexibility | 10 | Matches your location and hours rules | Minor friction | Conflicts with a hard rule |
| 5 | Management Style | 8 | Manager has a record of developing people | Unknown or mixed | Known pattern of churn under this manager |
| 6 | Team Structure | 8 | Team exists, roles are clear, hiring plan funded | Team partly built | Solo with no path to a team |
| 7 | Impact & Visibility | 8 | Your function is tied to results and seen by leadership | Reported on, rarely discussed | Your function is treated as a cost line |
| 8 | Company Stage & Size | 8 | Inside your preferred stage and size | Slightly outside | Far outside, such as a mega-cap or a pre-product startup |
| 9 | Mission & Industry | 7 | Operational mission you care about | Neutral | Conflicts with your values |
| 10 | Career Trajectory | 6 | Clear next step and a company on the way up | Flat | Shrinking or stalled |
| 11 | Product & Buyer | 5 | You know the buyer and the product category | Adjacent | No relevant background |
| 12 | Recognition & Feedback | 4 | Visible praise and a feedback culture | Unknown | Public complaints about being unheard |

## Score bands and statuses

| Score | Band | Status to set |
|-------|------|---------------|
| 80 to 100 | Strong Fit | Pursue |
| 70 to 79.9 | Solid Fit | Pursue |
| 55 to 69.9 | Mixed Fit | Pursue with Diligence |
| Below 55 | Poor Fit | Pass |

A row you have already acted on (Applied, Followed Up, Call Scheduled, Interview) keeps its status whatever it scores. Your real progress beats the rubric.

## Research checklist per company

- The job description itself, from the company's own site or ATS
- Leadership backgrounds on LinkedIn
- Glassdoor or similar, last 12 months, looking for themes rather than single reviews
- Funding and headcount (Crunchbase, PitchBook, press)
- Mission and values pages, and whether the product actually delivers on them
- News and legal: lawsuits, layoffs, acquisitions
- Comp: the posted range, or a market estimate labeled as an estimate
- Deal-breaker checks that need digging: subsidiaries, the company's own careers page, contracts

## Evidence rules

- Cite a source for every factual claim in Criteria Met, Criteria Gaps and Sources.
- Label inferences as inferences.
- If a category cannot be told from the outside, score it 5 and say it is a placeholder in Notes. Rescore when you learn more.
