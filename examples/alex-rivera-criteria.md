# Dream Job Criteria (fictional example)

## The role

- Titles I want: Director of Support Operations, Head of Customer Support, Senior Manager Support Operations
- Level and scope: owns the support organization or its operations function. Reports to a COO or a VP of Customer Experience.
- Titles I do not want: Support Agent, Team Lead, Technical Account Manager
- Function mix I want to own: staffing and scheduling, tooling, quality, reporting. Escalations are fine, product feedback is a plus.

## The company

- Stage and size: 200 to 1,500 employees, past the earliest stage
- Funding or ownership I prefer: no preference
- Where larger or older companies land: flag on autonomy, do not reject
- Product and buyer I understand well: software sold to small and mid-size businesses

## Where and how I work

- Location rules: remote, or hybrid within 25 miles of Denver
- Travel: up to 10 percent
- Hours or time zone limits: overlap with US Mountain time for at least five hours

## Compensation

- Base floor: 150,000
- Target total comp: 185,000
- Equity expectations: nice to have, not required
- Exceptions: will accept 135,000 for a mission I care about

## Mission

- Missions I am drawn to: education access, public health
- What counts as real: the product itself does the good, not a donation program

## Deal-breakers

- Any revenue from tobacco or gambling, including through a subsidiary
- A pending lawsuit from former employees alleging retaliation or discrimination by the company itself. A company suing a competitor does not count.
- A support team that has turned over its whole leadership more than once in 18 months

## Nice to have

- A support tooling budget that is already approved
- A named owner for customer data and reporting

## Not a target

- Roles that are mostly outsourced vendor management
- Companies where support reports into sales

## Search terms

- "Director of Support Operations", "Head of Customer Support", "Senior Manager Support Operations"
